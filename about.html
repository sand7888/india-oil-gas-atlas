About & sources

Where the numbers come from

Source

All figures come from the Oil and Gas Snapshot of States, a quarterly report by the Petroleum Planning & Analysis Cell (PPAC), Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. The report is published on ppac.gov.in.

Vol. 2026-27, Edition IApril–June 2026 · published 13 August 2026

The oil balance also uses PPAC's monthly Import/Export of Crude Oil and Petroleum Products (2026-27), compiled from oil companies and DGCIS, and its monthly Indigenous Crude Oil Production, compiled from oil companies and the Directorate General of Hydrocarbons (DGH). These figures are provisional and are revised as PPAC issues new months; the latest months are pro-rated estimates.

This site is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by PPAC or the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas. For official figures, refer to the original report.

Notes for this edition

  • Figures are as of 30 June 2026 unless a note below says otherwise.
  • Total and rural population are Registrar General of India projections for 1 July 2026.
  • SGDP (constant prices) and per capita income are for FY 2024-25, except Goa, Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim, Chandigarh and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands (FY 2023-24) and Gujarat (FY 2022-23). Source: RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States.
  • City gas infrastructure (geographical areas, CNG stations, PNG connections) is as of 31 May 2026, as published by PNGRB.
  • State-wise gas production and consumption are provisional. CNG and PNG sales are apportioned using PPAC's data for the second half of FY 2025-26.
  • Production, consumption and sales of petroleum products are monthly averages for April to June 2026.
  • Petrol and diesel retail prices and effective VAT rates are as of 1 July 2026. VAT and SGST collections cover FY 2025-26, as of 31 March 2026.
  • A city gas area that spans two states is counted once, under one state chosen by PPAC (for example, areas shared by Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka and Tamil Nadu are counted under Andhra Pradesh).
  • India's total gas consumption includes 58.04 MMSCM used in cross-country pipeline transport that is not assigned to any state.

How the data was prepared

  • Each state's two-page table was read from the PDF by the position of every number on the page, then checked so that all 37 tables (India, 28 states, 8 union territories) carry the same 59 measures.
  • Values shown as “-” or “NA” in the report are treated as no data, not zero.
  • Fuel consumption on the map is PPAC's figure for all petroleum products, reported as a monthly average in thousand tonnes. The map multiplies it by 12 and shows it in million tonnes a year, the same unit as refining capacity. Because it is based on three months, it does not account for seasonal swings over a full year.
  • Per lakh people divides a state's figure by its total population in lakh. It is offered only for totals such as volumes and counts, not for prices, percentages or figures that are already per person.
  • Map colours split the states that report a value above zero into five bands with roughly equal numbers of states in each. States reporting zero are grey; states with no data are hatched.
  • Oil balance sets India's crude imports and domestic crude against what refineries process, and product imports against consumption and exports, all as monthly averages for the same three months multiplied by 12. Imported LPG is counted towards LPG use and other imported products towards other products; diesel, petrol and LPG use come from the Snapshot, and the rest of consumption is shown as other products. The striped flow labelled refinery fuel, losses and stock changes is the remainder once consumption and exports are subtracted from supply; it also absorbs differences between the two sources. LNG is not included.
  • Domestic crude is split by producer: ONGC and Oil India's nomination blocks (fields allotted to them by the government), contract blocks run under production- or revenue-sharing contracts (PSC/RSC), and condensate, a light liquid recovered with natural gas. Together they match the Snapshot's crude production figure.
  • Barrels: crude oil and refinery capacity are also shown in million barrels a day (mb/d), using the standard average of 7.33 barrels per tonne of crude and 365 days a year. Actual crudes range roughly from 7 to 7.5 barrels per tonne depending on density. Products are shown in tonnes only, because their densities differ widely (a tonne of LPG is about 11.6 barrels, a tonne of fuel oil about 6.7).
  • Ranks are out of the states and union territories with a value; tied values share a rank.
  • Share of India divides the state's figure by the India total in the same report.

Units and terms

TMT
Thousand metric tonnes
MMTPA
Million metric tonnes per annum (refinery or terminal capacity)
mb/d
Million barrels a day (1 barrel = 159 litres)
MMSCM
Million standard cubic metres (natural gas)
KL
Kilolitres
Lakh / Cr
1 lakh = 100,000; 1 crore = 10 million
POL
Petroleum, oil and lubricants: all petroleum products combined
MS / HSD
Motor spirit (petrol) / high-speed diesel
LPG
Liquefied petroleum gas (cooking gas)
CGD / GA
City gas distribution / geographical area licensed for it
CNG / PNG
Compressed natural gas (vehicles) / piped natural gas (homes, shops, industry)
PMUY
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana, the scheme for LPG connections to low-income households
DBC
Double-bottle connection (two LPG cylinders)
EBP / ESY
Ethanol Blended Petrol programme / ethanol supply year (November to October)
RSP
Retail selling price, in the state capital
SGDP
State gross domestic product
